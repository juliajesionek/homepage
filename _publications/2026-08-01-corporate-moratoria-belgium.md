category: work_in_progress

date: 2026-08-01
authors: "with Yasin Kürşat Önder and Koen Schoors"
note: "Can debt suspension stabilize firms without using fiscal space? Using proprietary BNP Paribas Fortis credit data matched to BELFIRST balance sheets, I exploit the predetermined delinquency-based eligibility cutoff of Belgium's 2020 corporate moratorium in a fuzzy regression discontinuity design. Preliminary results show that firms receiving the suspension raised short-term liabilities and, with a delay, increased investment."
