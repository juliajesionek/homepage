figure_points:
  - "In April 2020, Belgium introduced a debt suspension policy aimed at supporting mortgage holders facing financial challenges due to the COVID-19 pandemic. Approximately 6% of outstanding mortgages benefited from the program."
  - "This study examines the impact of the mortgage moratoria program on the financial outcomes of households during the pandemic, arguing that assignment to the policy provided critical support to the most vulnerable borrowers."
  - "Findings show that eligible households for the debt suspension policy reduced their mortgage payments by an average of €307 relative to the non-eligible during the policy period."
  - "While no immediate impact on consumption was observed, eligible borrowers increased spending on services compared to non-eligible households after the policy ended."
